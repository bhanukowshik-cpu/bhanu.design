# City scroll — storyboard brief for ChatGPT

Paste **Message 1** first, then each frame as its own message in the same chat,
so ChatGPT keeps the city, camera style and palette consistent.
Ask for **16:9** images unless the frame says otherwise.

---

## Message 1 — the brief (paste first)

I'm a design engineer redesigning my portfolio homepage. Help me storyboard one
scroll-driven experience. Don't write code; I need images.

**The idea:** the homepage is a stylized 3D city at dusk, inspired by San Francisco
(hills, the bay, fog rolling in, a bridge in the distance), but it's my own city,
not a map. Each building is a product I've built. The bigger the building, the
bigger my role. When the page loads the city is dark. As the visitor scrolls, the
camera glides from building to building and each one's windows switch on, floor
by floor. Buildings already passed stay lit, so the skyline fills up as you scroll.
The last and tallest building, my company Verified Call, lights up brightest, with
a rooftop sign.

**The buildings:**
- **Verified Call**: the tallest tower, glass and steel, HQ of the company I founded. A soft green verification glow at the top. Rooftop sign.
- **EverTutor**: a mid-size modern campus, two linked blocks with a courtyard (an AI tutoring company, 2,000+ students a day).
- **Stressie**: a small, warm studio building with plants on the terraces (a wellness app).
- **Dearly**: a tiny corner shop with a striped awning, like a letter shop (voice letters, built in 48 hours).
- Empty lots with construction cranes: projects still to come.

**Visual style (keep it the same in every frame):** stylized 3D miniature, like a
high-end Blender diorama, with soft, clean low-poly shapes, not photoreal. Palette:
deep navy and blue-black sky, cool moonlit blue on the buildings, warm amber
window light (#FFC98A), and one accent of pale green on Verified Call. Light fog,
subtle bloom, cinematic. Lots of empty sky at the top of the frame for headline
text. No people in close-up. Keep signs simple; real text will be added in code.

Confirm you understand, then I'll ask for frames one by one.

## Frame 1 — Arrival (hero, nothing lit)

Frame 1 of 5: the first screen. Wide three-quarter aerial view of the whole city
at deep dusk, almost all windows dark, a few streetlights only. The sky takes the
top 45% of the frame and is empty for a big headline. Fog in the low streets, the
bay on one side, a bridge silhouette far away. The Verified Call tower is visible
but dark, slightly right of centre. Calm, quiet, expectant.

## Frame 2 — First light: EverTutor

Frame 2 of 5: the camera has glided down and closer to the EverTutor campus
(two linked blocks with a courtyard). Its windows are switching on floor by floor:
the lower floors are fully lit warm amber, the top floors still dark. The rest of
the city is still dark and slightly out of focus. Leave the right third of the
frame calm and dark for a project card.

## Frame 3 — The skyline fills: Stressie and Dearly

Frame 3 of 5: the camera has moved along the street. The small Stressie studio
(plants on terraces) is now lit, and just ahead the tiny Dearly corner shop
(striped awning) is glowing. Behind, the EverTutor campus stays lit. You can see
a trail of lit buildings behind the camera and dark ones ahead. Leave the left
third of the frame calm for a project card.

## Frame 4 — The tower: Verified Call

Frame 4 of 5: low-angle view looking up at the Verified Call tower as it lights
up. Every floor glows, brighter than any other building, and a soft pale-green
glow crowns the top where a rooftop sign would be. Fog swirls around its base.
The lit buildings from earlier are visible small in the background. This is the
climax: confident, slightly heroic.

## Frame 5 — The whole city (pull back)

Frame 5 of 5: the camera has pulled back to the wide view from Frame 1, but now
the whole city is lit warm amber, with Verified Call the brightest, and the
empty lots have cranes with small blinking lights (future projects). Sky empty at
the top for a closing line. It should feel like an ending and an invitation.
