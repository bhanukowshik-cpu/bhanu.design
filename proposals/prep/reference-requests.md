# Reference requests (drafts for Bhanu to send himself)

Why: every AI review so far says the same thing: the work is strong, but outcomes are "reportedly". A short named
quote or a public LinkedIn recommendation from someone who saw the work turns "reportedly" into "confirmed by".
What gets used: name, title, and 1–3 sentences on the site next to the claim they confirm, only with their OK.

Tip: send on LinkedIn (a recommendation there is public and checkable, which is what AI reviewers trust most).
Fill in the [brackets]. Each message is under 80 words.

---

## 1. EverTutor founder / CEO — confirms role and scale

> Hi [name], hope things are going well at EverTutor. I'm putting my portfolio in front of a few senior design
> roles, and reviewers keep asking for someone who saw the work first-hand. Would you be open to writing a short
> LinkedIn recommendation, or 2–3 sentences I can quote with your name? Most useful would be my role (sole product
> designer on Live and Studio) and what you saw change. No pressure at all, and happy to send a draft to edit.

What it would confirm on the site: sole product designer; ~2,000 learners across 8+ schools; the whiteboard-first
tutor experience; Studio's 104 h → ~4 h lesson time.

## 2. A teacher or content designer who used Studio — confirms the 104 h → 4 h change from the user's side

> Hi [name]! I'm writing up the EverTutor Studio work for my portfolio. Since you were one of the people actually
> building lessons in it, would you be up for 1–2 sentences on what changed for you, before vs after? I'd quote it
> with your name and role, only if you're comfortable. Thanks either way, it was great building it with you.

What it would confirm: lesson-building time and effort before vs after; that teachers kept control of how the
tutor teaches.

## 3. Stressie founder — confirms the pilot and his role

> Hi [name], I'm sharing the Stressie work in my portfolio and want the numbers to come from you, not just me.
> Would you be open to a short LinkedIn recommendation or a 2–3 sentence quote on my role (founding designer,
> Figma to SwiftUI) and the V2 pilot, where daily engagement went from 12% to 56%? Happy to send a draft you can
> change freely.

What it would confirm: founding designer; built the iOS app in SwiftUI; the V2 pilot engagement change; its role in
the funding.

## 4. Optional: an engineer to re-run the Verified Call evaluation kit — independent validation

> Hey [name], I published part of Verified Call's evaluation system as open code
> (github.com/bhanukowshik-cpu/verified-call-eval-kit): exact confidence bounds, rules pinned before results, and a
> claim guard for product copy. Would you be up for running it and telling me honestly what's weak? If it holds up,
> I'd love a line I can quote; if it doesn't, I'd rather know.

What it would confirm: that someone outside checked the method (the smallest form of independent validation).

## If they say "send me a draft"

Keep the draft factual and let them change anything. Example for #1:

> "Bhanu was the sole product designer on EverTutor Live and Studio. [One sentence on what they saw him do, in their
> words.] The product reached about 2,000 learners across 8+ schools."

Never publish a quote they haven't approved in writing.
