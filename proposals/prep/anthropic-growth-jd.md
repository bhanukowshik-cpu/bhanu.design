# Anthropic — Product Designer, Growth (San Francisco) — job description as pasted by Bhanu, 13 Nov 2026

Growth at Anthropic: helping people discover what Claude can do and make it part of how they work, from first session to company rollout. Small design team across all products; foundational changes and new product experiences over small optimizations.

Senior Product Designer on Growth owns how Claude grows with people who use it for everyday work: why some people make Claude a habit and others don't, find bottlenecks, lead product changes; set vision; partner with product, eng, data science; raise the quality bar. Thrive in ambiguity; sharp diagnostic judgment + exceptional craft; clear POV from messy signals; prototype quickly; drive alignment; use Claude Code.

Responsibilities: own growth for everyday-work use end to end; diagnose habitual use and blockers (data, research, hands-on); opinionated vision → roadmap; redesign core parts (onboarding, first-run, everyday workflows); shape hypotheses and experiments, judge results with nuance beyond topline; exceptionally crafted interactions; alignment with partner teams, individual → team → company adoption; mentor designers; prototype with Claude Code and AI tools.

Minimum: shipped portfolio with vision and craft; owned growth for a major area end to end; deep growth design (activation, onboarding, retention, engagement, expansion) at meaningful scale; designed/launched/interpreted experiments; product analytics fluency (funnels, retention, engagement); Figma + Claude Code; collaboration across PM/eng/DS.

Preferred: growth strategy across teams; enterprise/B2B SaaS/productivity/collaboration; PLG single user → team → company; mentoring; SQL; front-end prototyping; defining AI workflows for self/team; POV on when a product should ask for attention vs stay out of the way; passion for safe, beneficial AI.

Representative projects: diagnose daily-use vs drift; redesign a core part so people return more; first-run vision through experiments to a permanent flow; path from one person's daily use to team adoption.

Comp $305K–$460K. Hybrid 25% in office. Candidate AI guidance: live interviews without AI assistance unless permitted.
